# GodotTogether Code of Conduct

GodotTogether aims to be a universal project made for everyone, by everyone.
It does not aim to engage in politics or favor or discriminate any groups of people.

## General rules
### Be kind
Be kind and respectful to everyone.
Stay calm and don't engage in arguments.

Show empathy towards those who are confused.

If someone doesn't exactly follow some instructions, guide them through.

### Treat everyone equally
Do not discriminate against any individuals or groups of people, but do not favorize them either.
Contributions shall be rated by their quality and achieved task, instead of the characteristics of their author.

### No politics
Please don't engage in politics in any way, as that goes against the project's goal of neutrality.
Such software is not a right place for politics.

### Don't spam or make joke contributions
Opening issues and pull requests that serve no purpose or intentionally harm the codebase, are not acceptable.

Examples bad issues include:
- Does GodotTogether support smart microwaves?
- Man I want to eat my ceiling so bad
- Meow

Example bad PRs include:
- Prefixed every function and variable with `the_`
- Cleanup: deleted every single file

**Easter eggs** however **may be allowed** in small amount.

### Don't troll
Simply respect everyone's time, don't say things that aren't true and don't make them do something harmful.

## Enforcement
If you violate a rule, you may:
- Receive a worded warning
- Have your comment, issue or pull request closed or deleted
- Be temporarily restricted from contributing (opening issues, creating pull requests)
- Be forever restricted from contributing
